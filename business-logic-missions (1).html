Discount/Coupon Stacking

🎯 Objective

Dekho multiple discounts (coupon + gift-card + points) ek saath ek order pe lag sakte hain kya.

🧠 Kyun hota hai
  • Developer har discount ko alag-alag test karta hai — saath-mein-use hote dekhne ka socha hi nahi
  • Backend har apply-request ko independently process karta hai
  • Ek shared total pe baar-baar minus karta jaata hai, isliye teeno ek saath chal jaate hain
🔍 Kahan dhoondo

Checkout page jahan coupon + gift-card + points teeno box ek saath dikhein

📡 Normal
Coupon "WELCOME20" apply
₹1000 → ₹800
⚠️ Stacked
+ gift-card ₹200 → ₹600
+ 300 points → ₹500
(sab ek saath)

👉 Farak: teeno discount alag-alag test kiye to sahi the — par teeno ek saath bhar ke submit karne se total ₹500 reh gaya, jo akele-akele allowed tha wahi saath-mein-use galat ban gaya.

🧪 Challenge

2 discount-mechanism dhoondo, dono ek saath apply karke final-total check karo.

🏁 Mission Complete — jab 2 discounts stack karke dikha sako.
