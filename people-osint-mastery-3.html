Username Recon — step by step

  1. Target ka ek known username collect karo (kisi bhi platform se)
  2. Sherlock/Maigret CLI chalao: python3 sherlock.py targetuser --print-found
  3. Har result manually visit karo — parked/fake accounts filter karo
  4. Username variations bhi try karo (numbers add/remove, underscores)
  5. Bio, linked links, profile pic — sab ek jagah note karo
  6. Same username across platforms = strong signal ki same person hai

  2. Sock puppet account banao — koi real personal detail (real naam, real photo, real location) mat daalo

🔺 Correlation — Sab Data Ek Profile Mein

Final identity profile banane ka process

  1. Ek document/spreadsheet banao: Username(s) | Email(s) | Phone | Social profiles | Photos | Location clues | Employer/Company
  2. Timeline banao — kab kaunsi info post hui, kab account bana
  3. Cross-verify karo — kya email aur phone same platforms pe registered hain? (confirms same person)
  4. Contradictions note karo — agar do sources conflict karte hain to dono ko flag karo, confirm mat karo bina verification ke
  5. Confidence level assign karo har data point ko — "confirmed", "likely", "unverified"
  6. Final report mein sirf verified/high-confidence findings include karo
Cyber cell investigations mein evidence chain important hoti hai — har finding ka source aur timestamp record karna zaroori hai, sirf assumption pe kabhi conclude mat karo.
Sirf authorized investigations, legal research, ya khud ki bug-bounty scope ke andar use karo. Private individual ko bina authorization track karna Indian law ke under offence hai.
